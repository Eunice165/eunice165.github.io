Hello! My name is Eunice, and I am currently an undergraduate student at the University of California, Riverside, majoring in Anthropology. I have always been interested in learning about people, different cultures, and the experiences that shape how we understand the world around us. Throughout my academic journey, I have become particularly interested in medical anthropology and public health, especially the ways culture, history, and social factors influence people's experiences with healthcare. One of the things I enjoy most about studying anthropology is that it allows me to look at everyday experiences from different perspectives. I find it interesting how people's backgrounds, environments, and communities can influence their beliefs, opportunities, and access to resources. I hope to continue exploring these connections throughout my education, particularly in relation to healthcare accessibility, health disparities, and the experiences of different communities.

Outside of my coursework, I have gained experience in several different work environments, including customer service, childcare, and education. These experiences have helped me become a better communicator, develop patience, and learn how to adapt to different situations. One experience I particularly enjoyed was working as an instructor for a summer educational program, where I taught elementary and middle school students about astronomy and space science. It was rewarding to introduce students to new topics, encourage their curiosity, and find creative ways to make learning enjoyable. Although my primary academic interests are rooted in the social sciences, I also recognize the importance of developing skills outside of my major. Taking computer science has given me an opportunity to become more familiar with technology and understand how it can be applied in different academic and professional fields. I believe that technological literacy, problem-solving, and the ability to work with digital tools are valuable skills regardless of one's career path. I am always open to learning something new, especially when it allows me to approach familiar subjects from a different perspective.

Outside of academics and work, I enjoy listening to music, watching films and television, and exploring topics that interest me. I would describe myself as someone who is naturally curious and enjoys learning about things that challenge my perspective. I also value creativity, personal growth, and taking the time to understand ideas beyond what is immediately presented. As I continue my education at UC Riverside, I hope to gain more experience, strengthen my skills, and explore opportunities that align with my interests in anthropology, healthcare, and public health. Ultimately, I want to pursue a career that allows me to continue learning while contributing something meaningful to others. I look forward to seeing where my academic and professional journey takes me.
